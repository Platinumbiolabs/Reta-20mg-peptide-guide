Research Background

Reta (retatrutide) is an investigational compound studied for its interaction with three metabolic hormone receptors: GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon.

Its multi-receptor activity has attracted significant attention in metabolic and endocrinology research, making it an important subject of ongoing scientific investigation.
